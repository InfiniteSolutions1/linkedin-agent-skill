The speed-to-lead
playbook

Your new lead isn't waiting. They're filling out forms with other firms. This page shows what to measure, and how to take follow-up off your agents' memory and put it on rules.

Step 1 · Measure it

  • Minutes from form or ad lead to first call, for every source: Google Ads, Meta, LSA, web forms.
  • Leads never called this week.
  • Attempts per lead before the team gives up.
  • Contact rate: leads reached ÷ leads received.
  • Sign-up rate by response time: called within 5 minutes vs. after an hour.

Step 2 · Set the rules

  • One owner for speed to lead, reviewed every week.
  • New leads route instantly to the dialer, not to an inbox.
  • The cadence is written down, and it changes by rule, not by mood.
