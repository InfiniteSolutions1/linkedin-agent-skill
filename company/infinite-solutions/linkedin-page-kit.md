### Post 2: How we work

```
We don't start an engagement by recommending software.

We start with a question: where does a case get stuck?

For a 3-attorney PI shop it's often the 7pm call that goes to voicemail. For a 60-person firm it's records and liens stalling in pre-litigation. For a 300-person firm running 6 practice areas, it's that nobody can say which campaign produced which signed case.

Each one needs a different fix. A new platform rarely helps until you know which one you're fixing.

So every engagement runs the same three steps:

Assess. Map demand, intake, matter flow, tools, reporting and ownership.
Design. Define the workflow, the controls, the automation and the scorecard.
Operate. Help implement it, then keep a weekly cadence until your team runs it without us.

Everything gets built in your systems. You own it.

Which stage costs your firm the most cases right now: intake, pre-lit, or attribution?

The assessment is free, signed under NDA first, and we reply the same business day.

#LegalOperations #LawFirmManagement #LegalTech
```

### Post 3: The AI intake workflow

```
One intake call, from ring to review:

1. The call is transcribed.
2. AI fills the case management fields from the conversation.
3. High-value cases get flagged to your team by Slack or SMS.
4. A person makes the decision.

Step 4 is the part that matters. AI does the typing and the sorting. Your intake team keeps the judgment, and the attorney keeps the call.

What changes is speed and visibility. Nobody re-keys a 20-minute call into five screens. A strong case doesn't wait in a queue behind three that don't qualify. Leadership can see what came in and what happened to it.

It runs inside the systems you already have, and your firm owns it.

If you're evaluating AI for intake, start with this: what do you want a person to decide, and what do you never want a person to retype?

#LegalAI #LawFirmIntake #PersonalInjuryLaw
```
