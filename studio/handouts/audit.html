The signed-case
marketing audit

Your agency reports clicks. Your intake team reports leads. Your case management system holds the signed cases and fees. This checklist shows whether anything connects them. Every box you can't tick is money you can't see.

1 · Tracking

  • Every call is tracked by source. Unique tracking numbers for Google Ads, LSA, Meta, website, referral and TV.
  • Every form fires a conversion in GA4, with the source and campaign attached.
  • UTM tags are consistent on every ad, email and social link.

2 · Attribution

  • Your CRM stores the lead source on every inquiry, not just on signed cases.
  • Signed cases and fees tie back to the campaign that produced them.
  • Signed-case data is sent back to Google Ads and Meta (offline conversions), so they optimize for clients, not clicks.

3 · Spend

  • You know cost per signed case by campaign, not just cost per lead.
  • Search terms are reviewed every week and terms that never sign are added as negatives.
  • Meta lead-form quality is checked against sign-up rate, not volume.
  • LSA leads are reviewed and disputed when they don't qualify.

4 · Visibility: SEO, GEO, AEO

  • Google Business Profile is complete, with current reviews and practice areas.
  • Practice-area pages answer the question first, in plain language.
  • Ask ChatGPT, Gemini and Perplexity "best injury lawyer in [your city]." Do you appear?
